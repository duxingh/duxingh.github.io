[*How to Read a Book*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0671212095) by Mortimer Adler & Charles van Doren is a book first published in 1940 and substantially revised in 1972. It argues that we should read different books at different speeds, and it divides the reading process into four levels.

## Preface

For different writings, we should read at different speeds.

## Part I. The Dimensions of Reading

### 1. The Activity and Art of Reading

> There is some feeling nowadays that reading is not as necessary as it once was. Radio and especially television have taken over many of the functions once served by print, just as photography has taken over functions once served by painting and other graphic arts.

Every time a new technology of information breaks ground, there is always a doubt about the necessity of reading. In this AI era, there is a popular thought that asking questions to AI is a better way of learning than reading books.

#### Active Reading

The more active the reading the better.

### 2. The Levels of Reading

1. **Elementary reading**: As one masters this level one passes from nonliteracy to at least beginning literacy.
2. **Inspectional reading**: To get the most out of this book within a short time.
3. **Analytical reading**: To ask many organized questions of the book.
4. **Syntopical reading**: To read many books and place them in relation to one another and to a subject about which they all revolve.

### 4. The Second Level of Reading: Inspectional Reading

#### Inspectional Reading I: Systematic Skimming or Pre-reading

Inspectional reading is a process helping us decide whether it deserves analytical reading.

---

Here are the authors' suggestions about how to do systematic skimming:

1. *Look at the title page and preface.*
2. *Study the table of contents.*
3. *Check the index.*
4. *Read the publisher's blurb.*
5. *Look at some chapters that seem to be pivotal.*
6. *Turn the pages, dipping in here and there, reading a paragraph or two.*

#### Inspectional Reading II: Superficial Reading

Superficial reading is for reading a difficult book. It encourages reading through a book without stopping at any difficult point. After this process, you won't completely understand the book, but a half understanding is always better than no understanding.
