The material of [Brian W. Kernighan](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~bwk/)'s *Understanding the Digital World* comes from his course for non-technical students. It covers hardware, data, communication protocols, and the social impact on privacy and security.

## Part I. Hardware

### Chapter 1. What Is a Computer?

#### 1.1 Logical Construction

The logical construction of a computer consists of three main parts: a processor, memory or storage for instructions and data, input and output devices; a set of wires called a bus carries information between them.
